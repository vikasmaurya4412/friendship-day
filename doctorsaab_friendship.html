Knot 01

Through the Difficult Days

There was a time when things weren't going well for me at all. You didn't wait to be asked, and you didn't make it complicated — you were just there, exactly when it mattered.

I didn't have to explain everything for you to understand. That kind of presence, without conditions, is something I don't take lightly.

Knot 02

Through the Easy Days, Too

It's easy to remember who shows up during a crisis. But you were just as present in the simple, easy, uneventful days — the ones that don't get talked about but quietly hold a friendship together.

You didn't only exist for the hard chapters. You were part of the ordinary, good ones too, and that consistency means more than any single big gesture.

Knot 03

You Never Let Me Down

Whatever I needed, you helped in whatever way you could — sometimes with advice, sometimes just by being around, sometimes by doing more than you probably had to.

Not once did I feel like I was asking too much or that you'd disappear when it got inconvenient. That reliability isn't something I've found easily elsewhere.

Knot 04

The Days I Still Return To

I know life can't go back to being exactly how it was — people move, things change, days pass and don't repeat. But the memories from those old days haven't gone anywhere.

Every once in a while, something small brings them back — a place, a song, a random thought — and suddenly I'm right back there, missing those days all over again, grateful they happened at all.

Thank You — To You, and To Mahadev

For every difficult day you stood through with me, for every easy one you still chose to be part of, and for a friendship I'll carry with me no matter how much life changes around it.

Thank you, Mahadev, for placing this friendship in my life — and thank you, DoctorSaab, for never once making me feel alone in it.
